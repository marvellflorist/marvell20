What to expect

01

The Installation

Step into a burgundy floral interior created for MARVELL 20. At the center, a sculptural flower installation becomes the heart of the room - intimate, atmospheric, and designed to be experienced up close.

02

The Flower Counter

Discover a selection of single-flower bouquets, wrapped simply and made to be carried as a small piece of the bloom. Each piece is designed to feel light, personal, and easy to take home.

03

The Anniversary Drink

Extend the visit with a signature anniversary drink, created for the pop-up and served in limited custom cups. A small pause within the floral interior before continuing through the experience.

04

The Photo Stage

Capture the visit inside a dedicated photo area created for MARVELL 20. Designed with floral details, warm lighting, and a burgundy atmosphere, the photo stage gives guests a memory point within the experience.
